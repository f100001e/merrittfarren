About Merritt

Not a Politician.
A Problem Solver.

Merritt Farren has lived in California since the age of nine. He grew up in a middle-class family — son of a Navy pilot, attending public schools before earning his degree with honors from Stanford University and graduating from Berkeley Law .

With more than 30 years of experience in business, technology, and law , Merritt built his career at two of the world's most customer-centric organizations: at Disney , where he rose to Senior Vice President and General Counsel of the Disneyland Resort , overseeing Legal, Guest Claims & Security; and at Amazon , as Associate General Counsel for Media and New Technologies , where he played a key role launching Prime Video, Amazon Music, Kindle, eBooks, and Amazon Studios , and served as Chief Legal Officer for Audible . He also held senior roles in Corporate Development at Sony Pictures Entertainment .

When the fires destroyed his neighborhood, Merritt didn't just get his family back on its feet — he fought back. He formally intervened in State Farm's rate hike proceedings as an official consumer advocate , gaining a firsthand look at a broken system and a clear vision for how to fix it.

He is a father, a Californian, and a reformer — running not because it's easy, but because it's necessary.

Merritt is the only candidate for Insurance Commissioner with the experience in business, technology, and law required to solve our insurance crisis. Not a career politician looking for the next gig — a problem solver with the skills and motivation to deliver real results for California families and businesses.

Reform Agenda

The Plan to Fix
California's Insurance Crisis

California is the world's greatest center for innovation — yet we've failed to innovate where it matters most. Merritt's plan draws on real executive experience to deliver structural reform in home, auto, business, and health insurance — not political theater.

01

A Technology-Centric Reinvention of Insurance Regulations

No tinkering with a broken system. Merritt will lead big, from-the-ground-up reform — modeled on the teams that launched Kindle and eBooks — ushering in an insurance tech revolution that matches the innovation consumers enjoy in banking today.

California's insurance regulatory framework was built for a different era. Decades of incremental rule-making have produced a system that is slow, opaque, and hostile to competition. The answer is not another round of small adjustments to a fundamentally broken structure.

Merritt has been part of teams that launched entirely new categories at scale — Kindle, Prime Video, Amazon Studios, Audible. He knows what it takes to reinvent an industry from the ground up. That experience is what he will bring to the Department of Insurance.

The tech-centric reforms Merritt will implement recognize a fundamental truth: our rules need to work for both consumers and insurers. Insurance in California isn't working for either right now. With Merritt's leadership, it will work for both — and that insurance tech revolution will be built right here in California, bringing jobs and new business prosperity.

Tech Reform
02

Implement CAL Reinsure to Eliminate the FAIR Plan

A state-backed reinsurance authority removes concentrated wildfire risk from individual insurers — letting carriers re-enter California, restoring competition, and eliminating the FAIR Plan.

The core problem driving California's insurance crisis is that concentrated community fire risk has made it economically impossible for insurers to write home and property policies in large parts of the state. When an entire neighborhood burns, every policyholder files at once. No private insurer can absorb that — so they leave.

CAL Reinsure — developed with insurance industry input — moves that community-scale fire risk onto a reinsurance authority organized by the State. Insurers can then write policies statewide without fearing catastrophic community fire losses. Modeled on Florida's hurricane reinsurance program and the UK's Flood Re program, it has been proven to work.

CAL Reinsure also includes a non-negotiable requirement: full payment of the insured amount within 30 days of a total structural loss. Families cannot rebuild their lives while insurance companies delay. Neither can entire communities. The 30-day guarantee changes that — for individual homeowners and for the neighborhoods they need to rebuild together.

CAL Reinsure
03

Aggressive Leadership on Community Safety & Root Cost Drivers

Merritt will establish minimum community fire protection standards and go after the underlying costs driving prices up across home, auto, business, and health insurance — line by line.

Structural reforms and CAL Reinsure address the market collapse — but insurance will only become truly affordable when we attack the costs that drive prices up in the first place. Merritt will introduce a new Department of Insurance agenda focused on exactly that.

On community safety: Merritt will establish minimum fire protection standards that cities and counties need to meet to help ensure Californians are safe. Communities that invest in hardening and preparation will be recognized and rewarded in the insurance market. Those that don't will be held accountable.

On root costs: Merritt will work insurance line by insurance line — home, auto, business, and health — to expose and attack the underlying cost drivers inflating premiums. From infrastructure failures to fraud to lack of price transparency, the Department of Insurance under Merritt's leadership will shine a light on what is actually driving costs and pursue real solutions with unprecedented vigor.
